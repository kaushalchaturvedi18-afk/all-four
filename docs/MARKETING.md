# Marketing Manager

One card on the All Four home. It is the daily digital-marketing desk for a business that wants one post and a growth check, not another scheduler.

ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, and Grok are seats on this desk. They are roles. This repo does not call those companies.

## What a business wants

- One post they can approve in a minute.
- A next step: message, call, form, profile visit. Not a like.
- Proof they can stand behind. No invented numbers.
- Growth read from saves, clicks, and follows against the last log.
- Two channels run well. Five platforms is how the budget dies.
- A person still ships it.

## Industry bias this desk refuses

- Enterprise tool bias. Hootsuite and Sprout win roundups because of seat price and reviews. They are the wrong first buy for a one-post habit.
- Vanity metric bias. Reach flatters. Intent moves the business.
- Always-on bias. One post a day on the channel that already replies.
- Western B2B bias. LinkedIn-first advice misses markets where Instagram and WhatsApp close the work.
- Autopublish bias. Draft, then a person approves.
- Volume bias. More captions is not a strategy.

Prices move. Check the vendor page before you buy.

## How the four seats split a post

- ChatGPT — clarity. Result, then the instruction, then one action.
- Claude — care. Claims we can stand behind. No fake numbers.
- Copilot — build. Asset, alt text, link, approval, schedule note.
- Grok — edge. Cut the warmup. One offer. If it could be any business, rewrite it.

The desk stores approvals and growth logs in the browser. It does not publish.
